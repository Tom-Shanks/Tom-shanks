# Hey, I'm Tom.

I'm a Denver-based data and integration engineer, geographer, and a pretty obsessive data nerd.

I like building systems that turn observations of the physical world into something we can understand and use. That interest has taken me from geospatial analysis and disaster research to operational data engineering, RF sensing, satellite imagery, and aircraft telemetry.

Professionally, I work on complex data migrations and integrations for fire and emergency services software, taking messy source systems through validation, customer testing, and production cutover.

Outside work, I build things that listen to and measure the world around me.

## What I've been building

**[Satellite ground station](https://www.tomshanks.dev/projects/rfpi)**  
An automated Raspberry Pi and RTL-SDR station that predicts orbital passes, receives weather satellite transmissions, decodes imagery, and archives observations.

**[ADS-B aircraft telemetry](https://www.tomshanks.dev/projects/adsb-aircraft-tracker)**  
A receiver-to-PostGIS pipeline processing more than a million aircraft messages, with spatial analysis, APIs, and interactive flight-path visualizations.

**[Urban tree canopy classification](https://www.tomshanks.dev/projects/denver-urban-tree-classification)**  
LiDAR, aerial imagery, spatial machine learning, and geographic validation applied to urban vegetation.

**[Spatial Fields](https://github.com/Tom-Shanks/spatial-fields)**  
Interactive visualizations built from real satellite, aircraft, acoustic, and geospatial datasets, with source code and provenance notes.

## What interests me

Sensor integration, geospatial infrastructure, remote sensing, RF systems, space and ground software, autonomy, and the engineering required to turn imperfect observations into reliable information.

I'm particularly interested in systems that help us understand disasters, infrastructure, cities, and environmental change.
